experiments: unprimed adopter surfaces, mutation teeth, parser-checked docs

A method record that states the practice without relying on any product-specific
concept: publish a structurally fresh surface for an evaluator that cannot see the
project's assumptions; require it to declare its own evidence boundary; replay after
the fix. Prove a check can fail by mutating the real verified revision, not a
strawman, and demand satisfiability of every clause check. Parse the documentation
with the product's own reader, and seek the smallest differentiating cases. Close
with the two generalizable lessons about refusals and readiness boundaries.
